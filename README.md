# GarageKita

An iOS app built with SwiftUI during an Apple app development workshop (August 2026). GarageKita helps you keep track of your vehicles and everything that comes with owning them.

## Features

- **Home** – overview of your vehicles, upcoming tasks, active warranties and total spending
- **Garage** – add vehicles (with photos) and view their details
- **Care** – log maintenance and keep track of warranties
- **Tasks** – checklist of car-related to-dos with progress status
- **Emergency** – emergency contacts and nearby workshops
- **Profile** – manage your profile and invite family members

## Running the app

1. Open `Garagekita.xcodeproj` in Xcode on a Mac.
2. Pick an iPhone simulator (or your own iPhone).
3. Press **Run** (⌘R).
